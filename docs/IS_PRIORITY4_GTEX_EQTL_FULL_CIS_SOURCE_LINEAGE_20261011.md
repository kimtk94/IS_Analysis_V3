# IS priority 4 — GTEx reprocessed whole-cis universe and source-lineage audit

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-11 KST. **Research status:** full-cis tested variant denominator audit and reprocessed eQTL provenance check, **not a new coloc** or shared-causal-variant validation. Preserve original 2,225-gene discovery universe.

## What data were and were not obtained

1. The official GTEx v8 **original** tissue-level nominal all-associations archive is in a Google Cloud requester-pays bucket (the directory is publicly listed at ~462 GB). **No requester-pays export or full download was performed.** Its original complete tested cis SNP population remains inaccessible from the existing archived subset.
2. The distinct **EMBL-EBI eQTL Catalogue release 6 GTEx reanalysis**, study QTS000015 and tissue **QTD000161 / Brain – Cerebellar Hemisphere**, is openly served through tabix with region queries. The full-gene permutation summary `QTD000161.permuted.tsv.gz` was downloaded (~0.8 MB). Its `n_variants` field reports the number of SNPs tested across that gene's cis region in **this reanalysis**. All tests are NOT independently replicated associations.
3. The open indexed `QTD000161.all.tsv.gz` was queried exactly twice, on original archived BBJ–GTEx GRCh38 common SNP interval boundaries chr4:79,759,933–80,762,996 and chr10:103,079,395–104,199,932. Only nominal records belonging to the four stable ENSG gene IDs were retained. Duplicated dbSNP rsid rows were collapsed by exact chr:pos:REF:ALT. This resource uses the **ALT allele as the effect allele**.
4. Current original archived data consist of **1,694 chr4 or 1,583 chr10 variant–gene pairs per gene–tissue assay**, with exact GRCh38 keys and original QTL p/beta kept read-only. They represent the intersected BBJ GWAS and older GTEx QTL SNPs, **not an independently verified full tested cis universe**.

**Critical interpretation:** These fractions use the *reprocessed* Catalogue-tested full cis denominator, which is **not** the original GTEx v8 full-cis denominator. They quantify the narrow coverage of the archived GWAS–QTL intersection *relative to this distinct reprocessing*, not original missingness, bias-corrected replication rate or genome-wide candidate gene prevalence. Even within the archived GWAS interval, substantial testable SNPs lie outside the old matched overlap.

## Same genomic SNP ≠ same molecular QTL estimate

Of the 1,568/1,553 allele-exact overlapping SNPs per gene, the nominal p-values and effect sizes are not numerically identical between the original archived QTL source and QTD000161 reprocessing.

| Gene | Proportion abs(p_original − p_reprocessed)<0.001 | Beta sign concordance | Median absolute log10(p) difference | Reprocessed gene-level permutation p_beta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FGF5 | 0.89% | 77.2% | 0.467 | 0.0278074 |
| C4orf22 | 1.08% | 80.0% | 0.175 | 0.0005920 |
| CALHM2 | 2.12% | 78.7% | 0.196 | 0.0270452 |
| NEURL | 0.19% | 59.2% | 0.290 | 0.40605 |

The permutation p_beta is from the **reprocessed Catalogue**; it is not its genome-wide multiple-testing-adjusted eGene q-value nor a coloc PP.H4. These data should not be compared as if generated by a single study-analysis configuration. Differences may reflect processing versions, normalization, covariates, sample/variant filters, and phenotype coding, **not established error**. The same SNP position and allele identity alone cannot authenticate equal association statistics. Source selection and harmonization decisions must be explicitly documented.

## Study consequences

- Historical CALHM2 PP.H4=0.7977, FGF5=0.7787, NEURL=0.6776 and C4orf22=0.6599 are **legacy, numerically replayed ABF posteriors under their archived matched intersection and assumed p12=1e-5**, not evidence that the full eQTL-tested cis set was included.
- EAS504/EUR503 reference genotype LD coverage is available at the four genes but is not matched GTEx donor LD.
- **Do not automatically replace** old QTL p-values with Catalogue reprocessing numbers, use reprocessed n_variants as the original GTEx denominator, run coloc.susie on mixed source statistics, claim eGene status from permutation p_beta alone, or discard any of the 2,225 original positional candidate genes.
- Next defensible computational task: rebuild the *complete common per-study variant universe* with documented study-level tested variants, genome builds and allele conventions, using bounded reference public data; for formal multi-signal inference also resolve appropriate molecular sample cohort LD.
