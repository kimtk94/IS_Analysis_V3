### Critical source status correction

Earlier project notes used loose expressions such as “published 2026 Singh paper” and “source authors' reported high-effect DEG list”. In this manuscript:
- Call Singh et al. **the 2026 preprint** until a peer-reviewed article and DOI are separately verified.
- Call GSE313736 **the public dataset deposition**, not a verified journal-published sc-eQTL article.
- Call Mehrotra et al. the **2026 peer-reviewed journal article WITH a later erratum**, and label a pre-erratum source-supplement freeze with its SHA256 and retrieval provenance. The October correction is currently **unresolved** for effects.
- Correct the misleading GEO description “17 downregulated (log2 fold change <3)” **as an ambiguous threshold statement**; do not assert the authors used <−3 without their actual methods.
- Watch for inconsistencies in individual GEO GSM protocol fields (GSM treatment characteristic says 48h, whereas some growth-protocol prose references 3 days). Prefer the explicit treatment field and disclose the provenance ambiguity; do not claim all protocol text is perfectly consistent.

## 3. Actual analytical contribution vs scientific value

1. The 57-donor paired pseudobulk model is verifiable, but ~85.5% of the tested genome is BH-FDR significant: this does not license gene-by-gene pharmacological causality, especially when DEX/vehicle correlate exactly with distinct 10x libraries and BeamA labels are inferred after treatment.
2. Cross-study effects are **not numerically pooled**: original 500nM 48h donor paired edgeR log2FC; Mehrotra 100nM 48h DESeq2/published log2FC; GSE301517 500nM 7d log2((meanCPM_DEX+1)/(meanCPM_Vehicle+1)), equally weighted over three TM strains, no new 7d FDR. Thus signs and explicit caveats, not effect-size equivalence or dose-response, are the main figure.
3. The 55-donor dual annotation sensitivity is a real *methodological robustness* contribution: ~13,975 common testable genes, r=.94990493; MYOC/TGFBI/MMP2 same signed and BH-FDR significant under both. Within-source models share cells, donors and libraries; they are not independent replication.
4. The Hallmark EMT 124/194 triple direction statistic is *exploratory*, has correlated gene units and was analyzed after earlier results; it is not evidence of new canonical “EMT mechanism,” reduced outflow or actual ECM deposition.
