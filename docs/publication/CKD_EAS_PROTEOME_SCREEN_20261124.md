Do not label this the entire 7,000-protein 2026 atlas, or an independent cohort replication. The original paper includes direct Olink/SomaScan pQTL comparisons, PAV flags and platform colocalization; a later atlas https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scib.2026.10.060 has broad disease/trait MR analyses, whose renal endpoints must be audited before novelty claims.

## Freeze before outcome evaluation

* Distinct cis assay IDs constitute each platform's fixed denominator, including holds. In exposure-only source, require valid autosomal coordinate/alleles, finite beta/SE, positive SE, 0<EAF<1, INFO>0.3, P<5e-8 and F>=10. Conflicting duplicate variants held; identical duplicates collapse. Select maximum F (deterministic sentinel tie break) per assay. No substituting anchors after outcome access, even if unmapped/palindromic. Indels are held; reciprocal GRCh38–37 liftover and exact alleles required; palindromes held without outcome EAF.
* eGFR primary, BUN secondary/supportive; original Chen 2024 Figshare 24356587 complete gzip/MD5, source row counts and UCSC chain MD5 verified. Wald ratio and delta SE assuming zero exposure-outcome covariance, first-order P, worst covariance SE. Bonferroni per platform and phenotype, BH with all untestable assays padded by P=1 (exploratory, not a guarantee under arbitrary dependence).
* Published supplementary Data 1 UniProt/reagent mapping, strictly one unique edge for each UniProt and both assay IDs, no inference from similar names. Count same anchor and different anchor pairs separately, sign concordance, and joint significance. Same outcome, overlapping CKB participants, LD and binding effects preclude independent replication or formal platform heterogeneity; same-variant direction agreement can be mathematically induced. Rho from publication not a new correlation estimate.
* Store full per-assay and per-pair derived results as expiring Actions artifacts, summary includes denominator and attrition. No private data, no dense GWAS output committed, no automated promotion or main merge.

## Decision gates

Concordance and significance alone are not novelty. Compare all shortlisted UniProt IDs/genes with existing renal proteome MR, CKB atlas disease/trait results, kidney eQTL/pQTL papers and reverse MR. Prioritize independent kidney outcomes, regional pQTL colocalization with matched ancestry LD, protein-altering variant/epitope flags, assay specificity, and EUR vs EAS harmonized effects (not difference in significance). Existing 9-gene findings are not a valid untouched discovery confirmation. No scientific GO until those checks support the specific claim.
