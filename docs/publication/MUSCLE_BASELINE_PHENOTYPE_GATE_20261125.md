## Primary scientific question and novelty boundaries
Can a prespecified baseline muscle-expression program derived independently of long-term outcomes associate with subsequent continuous muscle growth and incremental response to exercise volume? Thalacker-Mercer 2013 GSE42507 already compares baseline transcriptomes in 44 extreme/modest/nonresponders after 16-week RT. Lixandrao 2025 DOI 10.1152/japplphysiol.00052.2025 GSE277819 selects nine participants per outcome-defined group from parental trial; 27 subjects, one baseline biopsy each, 1-set and 4-set contralateral 10-week RT, 81 RNA samples. Prior paper already tests PLIER acute-response pathways, protein turnover, response groups, etc. Parent-trial qCSA individual outcomes are available upon reasonable request per paper; no claim of public link until verified. Figure points not silently digitized or linked by response group/order. Separate continuous endpoints: MRI qCSA, myofiber CSA, lean mass. Do not count limbs or post samples as independent people. Outcome-selected samples cannot provide calibrated population prediction without sampling design, and high-dimensional ML with n=27 is inappropriate.

## Decision
GO only after exact baseline participant identifier to continuous outcome linkage, volume/side, baseline covariates, and non-overlap audit. If n=27 outcome-selected sample, limited exploratory association and uncertainty, not a population prediction model. With complete parental trial or independent sufficiently sized cohort, freeze one independently derived signed score before viewing response, use baseline only, participant-grouped analysis, compare clinical base, prespecified volume interaction with participant-level paired difference, uncertainty and held-out study validation. Post-training expression and group assignment cannot be predictors. If only responder categories, HOLD continuous-outcome prediction; no invented outcomes or pseudo-replication. No new private data or full historical smoke suite before commit; no main merge.
