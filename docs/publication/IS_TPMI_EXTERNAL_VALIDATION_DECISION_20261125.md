# Independent East Asian outcome validation decision — 2026-11-25

## Decision
Keep the 24 pre-existing regional markers and 192 exploratory contrasts fixed in PR #349. An external validation family is separate, predeclared before retrieving its effects. Do not select only significant ancestry contrasts. The primary question is same-allele association transportability to a Taiwanese cerebral infarction phenotype; this is not an external test of TOAST-subtype heterogeneity unless subtype-resolved outcome data are obtained.

## Locked proposed external test
1. Verify exact Phecode and ICD codes/exclusions with TPMI, cohort recruitment and overlap against GIGASTROKE, effect model and allele-frequency units, and legitimate public summary access. If not equivalent to AIS, call it related-phenotype transportability, not exact replication. If overlap unresolved, descriptive only.
2. Use the immutable 24 nonpalindromic regional markers and verified GRCh38 manifest from #349; include all 24 in the denominator, no replacement SNP or phenotype after outcome readout. Anchor direction is the archived ALT EAS AIS beta. Require unique exact rsID, coordinate, allele pair, valid signed beta/SE/P/EAF, and model scale. Record missing as HOLD. Do not infer effect allele from allele frequency alone.
3. Primary proposed external endpoint: directional two-sided association with Bonferroni 24 (P<.05/24 and concordant signed beta), conditional on source approval; show beta/CI for every evaluable marker and counts. For possible independent comparisons use signed differences only after comparable units and independence, retain distinct hypothesis family. No independent-locus count unless LD verified. This validation family overlaps earlier discovery and must not be presented as project-wide correction.
4. Do not test subtype interaction without corresponding subtype GWAS. No Coloc/MR, no causal gene or clinical claim. Stop if no approved source; request author-provided summary extracts for all 24 markers rather than reattempting identical downloads.

## 2026-11-25 follow-up — subtype replication explicitly separate
The full published Methods of the June 1, 2026 Neurology paper (DOI 10.1212/WNL.0000000000218126; PMID 42224635) explicitly state that deidentified TPMI data lack stroke subtyping. The all-IS GWAS used ICD-10 I63, I67, I68.2, I68.8 and I69.3 and excluded S06/C71, not the TOAST-defined LAS/CES/SVS phenotypes. Thus neither that all-IS analysis nor a general PheWeb infarction phenotype can directly reproduce the rs2767060 EAS-vs-EUR LAS contrast or the rs7203740 EAS SVS-vs-CES contrast. The study separately evaluated clinical/imaging TOAST classification in 716 IS patients from Taipei Veterans General Hospital. This is a candidate source for authorized subtype-level follow-up, not a verified publicly accessible GWAS or a certified nonoverlapping cohort for our markers. Its power, precise subtype counts, SNP availability, comparison group, effect scale and overlap with GIGASTROKE must be checked. The paper points to eMethods for data availability; access to exact marker/subtype extracts remains unconfirmed. Do not use the 716 as a replication N for a particular subtype or compare case-case coefficients directly with case-control contrasts.

Priority request if collaboration is feasible: the original frozen 24 C/T-or-other specified allele-pair markers, complete signed association estimates/SE/P/EAF by TOAST subtype with ascertainment, case/control definition, covariates, imputation quality, per-marker N and cohort overlap information. Prespecify endpoint and multiplicity before receiving these effects. No outreach sent, no individual-level data requested or accessed. If only within-case TOAST contrasts are available, use a separately specified compatible design and label as orthogonal rather than exact replication.

## Korean alternative — GENESIS-K and phenotype comparability

The 2026 ESOC abstract ESOC2026A1666, [GENESIS-K study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13146107/), reports prospective collection of clinical/genomic data with Infinium Asian Screening Array, 5,328 participants enrolled in 30 centers October 2023–November 2025, 99% ischemic stroke, and a target of at least 10,000. [KBDS study listing KSE102978](https://kbds.re.kr/KSE102978) describes plans for stroke genomic cohort and linkage. This is a candidate collaboration/data-access route, not a released summary-statistics resource. Post-2022 enrollment is promising for temporal separation from GIGASTROKE but does not certify absence of repeated patients or earlier cohort overlap. Stroke-free controls, case-control subtype effects, exact frozen variant genotypes or imputation, TOAST definitions, analysis covariates, selection weights (57% randomly selected in the abstract), model N, and authorization are unverified. If available only as cases, design an explicitly separate case-case contrast before readout. No direct contact or data request sent.

The Korean registry report (Do Yeon Kim et al., Hee-Joon Bae corresponding author, JKMS 2024, DOI 10.3346/jkms.2024.39.e278) reports LAA 38% in 2021, discussing intracranial arterial disease and MRI-based criteria that do not require the usual 50% stenosis threshold. This is clinically relevant ascertainment context, not genetic replication. Require harmonized definitions and separately specified ICAS/ECAS sensitivity where possible. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2024.39.e278). Maintain frozen marker family and verify cohort overlap before comparing effects.
