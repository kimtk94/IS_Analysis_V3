## Critical correction superseding earlier GSE313736 external-validation descriptions

GSE313736 is the discovery cohort, NOT a new independent validation cohort. This is directly established by main's docs/ocular/GATE_B_SELECTION_AUTHOR_SOURCE_AUDIT_20261031.md and docs/ocular/GATE_B_BEAMA_SELECTION_AND_AUTHOR_DEG_AUDIT_20261031.md, which identify the identical source metadata SHA256 41e4a6bf5792fe58e023b92f0129119dcf2b49877f62dd580b9dab5de48f5192, 169,398 rows and 59 paired donor/sample IDs. The original 57-donor BeamA edgeR analyses and pool-unit analyses of this same cohort were used in P5/P5d.

Trace the frozen 241-gene archive 14912728421 (run 45379596999, commit dad9e89c0ae55bdccae1d2d9366357b115da9401). The selecting script scripts/publication_sprint_followup.py reads the prior P5d archive 12995175346, both p5_all_gene_results.tsv.gz and p5d_poolunit_all_gene_results.tsv.gz, and a published separate TM 48h comparison workbook. Both discovery and external statistics entered the selection. This is NOT a blind holdout of the selection-stage 48h study, and the two GSE313736 models are not separate studies.

The later all-SNG GSE313736 workflow run 45418674704 (commit 3a5c160b3711aa660148c72a7d08f5e2f25b9304) is a **same-cohort cell-selection/annotation and aggregation robustness analysis**. Its old terminal status name NINE_POOL_FIXED_PANEL_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION is scientifically misleading and must be interpreted as SAME_COHORT_INTERNAL_ROBUSTNESS if produced. A pool-level sign-test P on a panel selected using this cohort is descriptive conditional on selection, NOT an independent confirmatory replication P, even if all nine pool means are positive. Do not tune panel, thresholds or exclude unfavorable pools. Prior proposed 59-pair metadata/feature QC remains valid. Exact retained cell intersection 166,323 (79,327 vehicle, 86,996 DEX), all 59 sample pairs retained at >=200 cells and >=80% retention. Missingness may be informative. Nine pools share arm-specific culture/library effects; no clinical steroid IOP response.
