## Ocular

The seven-day PRJNA729873 source includes 16 runs and eight donor-derived cell-line pairs ([NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/729873)). Previous DEE2 processing covers 13 runs, five complete pairs; all three missing pairs were nonresponders. Five positive pairs have minimum two-sided exact sign P=0.0625; Holm across the two frozen panels gave 0.125. For eight complete non-tied pairs, eight of eight positive yields P=0.0078125 (both panels Holm=0.015625); seven of eight positive yields P=0.0703125 before correction. This is a mathematical sensitivity calculation, not observed data or reason to discard negative pairs. Restore and uniformly re-quantify **all 16** with one validated annotation/pipeline and original FASTQ checksums; avoid mixing count pipelines only for missing arms. First get exact missing-run manifest and byte counts. No FASTQ downloaded in this PR.

The 16-hour PRJNA781014 was generated in a different country/center from the seven-day cohort ([Kathirvel et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-12443-7)); no causal duration contrast is identified. Six pairs alone cannot make both two-sided sign tests significant after Holm when all signs agree (minimum adjusted P=0.0625). Do not replace the fixed family after seeing results.

An additional descriptive cross-drug analysis is fixed before examining expression: [GSE16643](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE16643) uses two primary TM strains (86/93), 24-hour 1 uM dexamethasone (Dex), fluocinolone acetonide (FA), triamcinolone acetonide (TA) and DMSO controls; technical/culture repeats are not independent donors. [GPL6480](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GPL6480) probe annotations. Require exact sample accession and title identity, complete non-control probe values, unique exact gene-symbol mapping (ambiguous symbols excluded), median probe percentile rank per gene per array, mean within strain/arm, then signed treatment-minus-control shifts over the original frozen 241/16 genes from #335. Report coverage, actual number of arrays per arm and effect direction counts. Percentile changes are relative rank shifts, not log fold changes; no gene-level p values, no inference over 23 arrays, no clinical disease classification. This study is publicly known and prior repo use has not been exhaustively ruled out. Cross-drug agreement suggests a general glucocorticoid response, not necessarily Dex-specific or glaucoma-specific biology. A separately collected, adequately powered independent donor cohort remains necessary for confirmatory claims.
