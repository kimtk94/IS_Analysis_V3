# Decision-oriented protocols (proposed, not frozen)
Date: 2026-11-24. Refer to the novelty matrix for competing papers. These are internal decisions to test feasibility, not registered hypotheses. Existing outcomes are already seen; no retrospective claim of prespecification. Exact endpoint/effect thresholds must be agreed before analyzing a genuinely unused validation cohort.

## Ocular: first near-term paper candidate
- Question: Does the fixed 241-gene signed DEX response generalize to independent human TM donor pairs and across preparation/time, with quantifiable uncertainty? ECM16 is a secondary panel, not a discovery of ECM biology. Separate **general DEX response** from responder-by-treatment interaction and from clinical IOP prediction.
- Existing: [#335](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/pull/335), [paired run](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/actions/runs/45386503377). GSE301517 was previously examined; 7-day direction counts are descriptive, not blind holdout. PRJNA729873 has eight declared DEX/vehicle pairs; five complete DEE2 pairs (four responders, one nonresponder); both panels exact sign P=0.0625 and Holm P=0.125 as reported. Original 2022 time-course paper also uses PRJNA729873; 16h PRJNA781014 is a **candidate**, not assumed donor-independent.
- Next task A: construct accession/donor/condition crosswalk from original metadata and publications; distinguish reused donors, culture, platform, dose, duration and responder labels. Restore three missing arms using one harmonized quantification procedure for all 16 runs if public input and compute feasibility permit. No mixing uncalibrated processed counts. If not feasible, document missingness and limits, no identical transfer retries. This is completion of existing analysis, not new holdout.
- Preserve [the original fixed paired plan](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/blob/ef7d91bca31f8b10885a7908e34ed3e66125f095/docs/OCULAR_PAIRED_PANEL_VALIDATION.md): score, direction, exact two-sided sign test and Holm family for 241/16. At eight nontied pairs, 8/8 concordant gives P=0.0078125; 7/8 gives P=0.0703125. These are design arithmetic, not observed results. Report all eight or explicit missing/tied cases; no panel changes to achieve significance.
- Next task B: identify a genuinely unused, accession/donor-independent cohort. Freeze scale/normalization and one primary fixed-panel effect (paired donor signed score difference) and two-sided inference; report effect estimates/intervals and nulls, with stratified preparation/time analyses as secondary. Determine minimal meaningful effect and feasible sample size before outcome readout. Compare prespecified matched-expression random panels and canonical DEX positive controls without optimizing the panel on holdout.
- Do not fit a predictive responder model on eight pairs. Exploratory responder interaction only if credible participant-level sample size/covariates; no clinical IOP claims. If no independent cohort or effect is too uncertain, publishability remains conditional (possible transparent reproducibility report, not confirmatory mechanism paper).
- Figures: cohort provenance and attrition; donor-level paired effects with intervals; independent study effect forest / heterogeneity (only sufficiently comparable independent cohorts); gene direction/ECM descriptive map; negative controls and missingness.

## CKD: genetic priority
- Question: How portable are genetically proxied plasma protein–kidney associations between ancestries when comparable assays, variants and outcome definitions are available? Do not label UMOD/F12 as newly discovered.
- Existing [#333](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/pull/333): retrospective EUR selection, EAS outcome tests using same exposure anchor; UMOD/F12 survive all-13 correction; historical nine-locus ABF replay, SuSiE archive input reconciliation incomplete. No newly executed EAS exposure MR.
- Next task: CKB [PheWeb](https://pheweb.ckbiobank.org/) lists Olink traits but the portal announcement is not proof of downloadable regional pQTL. Check provider terms, the CKB 2025 Olink/SomaScan comparison (doi:10.1038/s41467-025-56935-2), the 2026 CKB atlas (doi:10.1016/j.scib.2026.10.060), and actual supplement/region fields. Create assay ID/UniProt/platform map, cis instrument/ancestry and full-region availability for the historical 13 EUR-selected assays, starting with UMOD/F12 as known benchmarks. Preserve denominators; do not treat unavailable EAS assay as null. No private CKD data on Actions.
- Qualification: shared protein target/reagent, effect units and phenotype eGFR scale, ancestry-specific EAF/build/effect allele, F statistic, LD, sampling covariance/overlap, appropriate cis-region coverage. Only compare directly if effects commensurate; otherwise descriptive portability, not ancestry heterogeneity. Investigate binding artifacts from protein-altering variants. Same EUR anchor applied to EAS outcome is not independent EAS exposure validation.
- Analysis: separate ancestry-specific strongest-F cis Wald primary and LD-aware multi-instrument sensitivity; estimate effects and SE, direct contrast with covariance if estimable, prespecified all-13 family including missing tests (conservative correction or predefined missing-data rule). ABF and multi-signal coloc with correct study inputs, prior grid and matched signed LD; reverse-direction analyses with validated independent instruments and appropriate multiplicity. eGFRcys/BUN/UACR/CKD support rather than interchangeable outcomes. Avoid treating coloc posterior as causality.
- GO if independent EAS exposure and outcome, comparability and adequate precision verified, and results differentiate portable vs unresolved associations in a way not already shown. HOLD if no EAS pQTL access or scale/overlap not identifiable; no imputation of N, LD or PP.H4. Stop generic repeated MR screen if closest literature already addresses the exact estimand.
- Figures: eligibility cascade, platform/ancestry comparison, effect forest with difference CI, regional signal/credible sets, phenotype specificity and sensitivity.

## IS: thesis core
- Question: Can one prespecified vascular/mural (COL4A2) or myeloid (SH3PXD2A) regulatory association be resolved beyond published stroke GWAS/pQTL evidence? Choose one by **source completeness and a documented literature gap**, not observed P. FGF5/ALDH2 are benchmark loci; rs671 is a coding/metabolic example, not forced eQTL mediation.
- Existing [#330](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/pull/330): 36 gene-by-tissue, 72 ancestry comparison cells source aggregates preserved, zero formal coloc for this group. Historical ABF results are exploratory, not current confirmation. [#281](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/pull/281): GSE331114 marker pilot lacks verified donor covariates/identities; no definitive differential expression.
- Next task: source-qualified one-locus feasibility table for both alternatives. Full regional EAS/EUR GWAS and cell-QTL beta/SE/alleles/build/ancestry, study N/scale/MAF as required, actual ancestry-matched signed LD, completeness and sample overlap; document which estimand is supported. Use official ImmuNexUT all-cis and other relevant sources only after row/identity/statistical validation. An allele count or expression donor count is not regression N. Signed-Z method is an alternative only with justified derived Z and complete LD/matched variant set, not an assumed substitute.
- Analysis: credible set, regional colocalization under prior/LD sensitivity, compare within same cell context where possible; assess power and direct differences rather than contrasting significance or H4 thresholds. Match EAS/EUR clinical definitions and subtype/case ascertainment. Independent cohort confirmation where non-overlap established. scRNA/ATAC for localization only unless donor-level counts/labels, disease-state confounders and sufficient replicates allow formal inference.
- GO if a specific unresolved mechanism has valid matched source, evidence robust to model/LD choice and independent support or defensible limited claim. HOLD if required molecular-QTL/LD unavailable; narrow to source-qualified regulatory localization without causal inference, or switch question after documenting gap. No automatic promotion of all 36 assays.
- Figures: eligibility, locus plot and fine-mapping, matched regulatory estimates with uncertainty, independent context, comparison limits.

## Muscle: redesign
- Question: Does a fixed, biologically justified acute resistance-exercise pathway signature measured at **baseline** predict subsequent continuous hypertrophy beyond clinical predictors? Do not claim prediction using post-training samples.
- [MUSCLE_MASTER](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/blob/main/docs/MUSCLE_MASTER.md): 20 suggestive RT loci, not conventionally significant; [#333](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/pull/333) GTEx hits are annotation, not GWAS replication. Gu 2026 strongest reported P=9.28e-7. MoTrPAC acute human multiomics preprint already covers temporal dynamics. GSE277819 original study uses paired legs with different training volume and responder labels; not independent limbs.
- Next: read official GSE277819 metadata and publication, verify participant IDs, baseline biopsies linked to continuous qCSA change, training volume, age/sex, both limbs and outcome availability; external chronic cohort candidate. Acute signature definition fixed from MoTrPAC without examining validation outcome, no sample mixing across species, cell/tissue or modalities.
- Analysis only if data support: participant as independent unit; mixed model for limb-level volume and participant, baseline signature, prespecified covariates. Participant-grouped nested resampling, all preprocessing/feature selection within training folds, comparison to clinical baseline; external locked-model evaluation for predictive claim. If sample too small, a small predefined score association and CI, not ML accuracy. Post-training association exploratory; GTEx and published lead SNPs secondary, no coloc from lead-only summary.
- GO only for genuinely nonduplicative question, sufficient participant-level data and independent test or honest limited association aim. HOLD if no linked continuous outcome or novel gap. No spurious prediction using response-defined groups.

## HCC: conditional
- One predefined RNA/protein discordance score (protein residual conditional on matched RNA, platform and tumor purity in training data) and externally validated clinical endpoint. Test incremental performance versus stage/etiology/clinical baseline with calibration and C-index or other endpoint-appropriate metric and interval. Confirm sample-level tumor/normal pairing, aliquots, endpoint timing, censoring and event counts; no leakage across split or batch. No fitting high-dimensional model to 48 patients without sufficient events. HOLD until independent matched cohort and new endpoint; prior work already describes discordance and prognosis.

## Oral cancer: redesign
- Puram GSE103322 already reports p-EMT and nodal context using five matched primary/LN pairs. Distinguish anatomical oral cavity from broader HNSCC, HPV/site and sampling effects. Confirm patient IDs and actual matched sample pairs. Use processed TPM only for appropriate per-patient summaries; raw counts for count-based pseudobulk require approved access. Define a program distinct from published p-EMT and validate in an independent oral-site cohort with patient-level paired inference. Do not compare pooled cells as independent, infer lineage from expression, or claim cell-cell causality. HOLD unless independent cohort and distinct gap available.

## Metabolic resilience: data-dependent
- Question: Which baseline genetic or environmental factors are associated with transition from MetS to sustained recovery or relapse, beyond known recovery–MACE associations? Approved KoGES individual-data linkage, consent, genotype QC, follow-up time and actual transition counts required. Primary multi-state / interval-censored transition model (or explicitly feasible alternative) with time origin, visit spacing, covariates and missingness; compare clinical baseline, report uncertainty. ## Decision semantics
CONDITIONAL_GO = worth investing in prerequisites, not permission to claim statistical significance or to submit. REDESIGN = current question overlaps known work or data cannot answer it. LINK_TO_IS = conceptual supporting analysis, not code merge. HOLD = unresolved feasibility/novelty. A valid null or non-estimable result must remain visible. No target-journal claim until evidence and scope match.
