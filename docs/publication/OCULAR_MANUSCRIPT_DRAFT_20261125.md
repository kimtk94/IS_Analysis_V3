# Ocular manuscript draft — 2026-11-25

## Proposed title
Cross-context directional consistency of a fixed glucocorticoid-response transcriptomic panel in human trabecular meshwork models

**Status:** working manuscript, conditional publication candidate; not submitted. Author list, affiliations, ethics/data reuse determination, journal selection, and visual inspection are pending. The analysis is retrospective; selection and evaluation data are not all independent.

## Abstract

**Background.** Glucocorticoid exposure alters transcription in human trabecular meshwork (TM). A practical question is whether a fixed response program retains its direction across experimental settings, rather than whether further genes can be selected from each new dataset.

**Methods.** A previously selected 241-gene response panel, including a nested 16-gene extracellular matrix (ECM) organization subset, was retained without refitting. The panel was derived from paired donor and pool-unit models of GSE313736 and published 48-hour TM statistics. Public datasets were assessed using equal-strain direction concordance or within-study direction-aligned expression scores. Analysis units, mapping, missingness and source conflicts were retained separately; no pooled inferential test or longitudinal model was used.

**Results.** In a seven-day TM culture dataset, all 241 genes mapped, of which 234 agreed with the expected direction in the equal-strain mean and 204 agreed in every strain. Both frozen panels had positive direction-aligned percentile shifts in each of five 10-day paired tissue samples (median +5.21 and +8.11 percentage points; Holm P=.125 for each). Conditional analysis of three commercial lots after 14 days of DEX yielded positive scores for both panels in all three lots (median +7.86 and +9.92 percentage points). Four of six sample source-name fields conflicted with their intended labels; the sole conflict-free lot was also positive. Cross-drug comparisons across two TM strains were directionally positive for both panels. These findings are descriptive, not independent confirmatory validation.

**Conclusions.** The frozen program shows directional consistency across heterogeneous experimental contexts. Unresolved sample identity, selection lineage, small numbers of biological units, missing nonresponders and platform differences preclude clinical prediction, causal interpretation or a quantitative time-course claim.

## Introduction

The trabecular meshwork participates in the regulation of aqueous humor outflow and intraocular pressure. Steroid exposure provides a widely used experimental setting in which to investigate outflow-related cellular responses. Prior work already demonstrates shared and drug-dependent transcriptional changes across glucocorticoids [1], alterations in methylation and gene expression during DEX exposure [2], and transcriptional differences in glucocorticoid responders and nonresponders [3]. Recent studies also characterize transcriptional responses across time and cell types [4] and connect glucocorticoid-associated chromatin remodeling with glaucoma-associated loci [5]. These studies limit novelty claims based solely on discovery of steroid-responsive genes, ECM biology or pathway enrichment.

Here we ask a narrower methodological and biological question: how consistently does a fixed, explicitly directional response panel behave when applied without re-selection to other publicly available experimental contexts? The analysis prioritizes transparent evidence accounting, exact source provenance, biological-unit separation and disclosure of unfavorable or incomplete results. It does not establish a biomarker of clinical steroid response or distinguish causal regulation from correlated expression.

## Materials and methods

### Design and panel lineage
This retrospective public-data reanalysis uses a previously frozen panel. GSE313736 is the selection/discovery cohort, not external validation. In the historical analysis, BeamA metadata classification at confidence >=0.75 and >=30 cells per arm retained 57 paired donor identities. Paired donor pseudobulk edgeR TMM robust negative-binomial quasi-likelihood analysis (design with donor/pair and treatment) and a pool-unit analysis supplied two views of the same cohort, not two independent studies. The exact historical counts/model scripts remain the authoritative methods sources; complete raw matrix lineage and dispersion/filtering details must be audited before submission.

The historical fixed-panel script joined 12,251 shared genes and used FDR <.05 in both internal models, concordant internal directions, absolute log2FC >=1 in both, and external 48-hour TM FDR <.05 with coherent direction to select 241 genes (117 expected up, 124 expected down). The ECM16 subset was defined from an exploratory Reactome overrepresentation analysis of this panel (seven expected up, nine expected down); it is nested, not an independent hypothesis. Pathway enrichment used exact cross-study symbols and an eligible mapped background, a 10–500 gene pathway-size range, BH across eligible terms including zero overlaps. Exact frozen archive provenance: run 45379596999, artifact 14912728421, SHA256 5ad39d6fb293130492e66c713ff7d155d03d752f31cac84c98f78c42671bd606. No panel changes are made for evaluation. Because published 48-hour data entered selection, it is not a held-out test. The temporal exposure text in GEO is not uniformly worded (48 hours in treatment fields versus three days in growth protocol in some records); check source publication and original workflow before asserting exact discovery exposure duration.

### Mapping and scoring
For microarray studies, exact platform-specific annotation and finite-value filters establish the eligible probe universe; ambiguous or multi-gene probe mappings are excluded according to each pinned implementation. Exact frozen candidate identifiers/symbols and unique Entrez mapping are required. For GSE65240, exact common candidate-to-Entrez mapping across GPL14550 and GPL17077 was fixed before scoring. For GSE16643, the source lacks an explicit CONTROL_TYPE field; the eligible annotated-probe sensitivity is not identical to a non-control probe filter. Report coverage for the complete panel and each direction. The GSE37474/GSE65240 gates require >=75% overall mapping and >=70% of each direction. No replacement genes, post-outcome exclusion or threshold tuning.

For each array/sample, rank eligible finite probe expression values with average ranks for ties; the percentile transformation is (rank - 0.5)/number of eligible probes. For a candidate with multiple eligible probes, take its median probe percentile. Within a verified pair, multiply treated-minus-control percentile by the frozen sign (+1 or -1) and average over mapped candidates. Report each study's pair-level median and mean; percentage-point shifts are 100 times this value, not fold changes. In GSE37474, a separate post-readout sensitivity first collapses eligible probe values within each Entrez gene before ranking the gene universe. In GSE16643, summarize technical array repeats within arm and strain as implemented in its source workflow; keep strains and drugs separate. RNA-seq direction/count-based effects are summarized in their original units, not converted to array scores.

### Source integrity and statistics
Record dataset accession, platform, exact file hashes, panel hash, matching denominators, source and output artifact identifiers. Match samples via explicit identity and treatment metadata, never by observed response or file order alone. GSE65240 title, explicit lot/treatment fields and original filenames agree for all six arrays; source_name is inconsistent for four. Preserve the source identity HOLD, with the explicitly conditional three-lot analysis and a separately displayed conflict-free lot 4973. Do not infer three independent donors or use a formal P in this context.

For the five verified GSE37474 pairs and five complete PRJNA729873 pairs, use two-sided exact sign tests per panel and Holm adjustment across the two panels within each study; sign counts do not imply independence across genes. Report leave-one-pair-out means for GSE37474. The paired tests are small-sample, retrospective descriptive assessments with no established blind validation. Missing PRJNA729873 arms concentrate in nonresponders; do not pool or impute them. Rank shifts, gene directional counts, strain means and log2 differences are different estimands; no cross-study meta-analysis, dose/time trend or pooled P value is performed. No inference of clinical IOP prediction.

## Discussion

The most defensible contribution is the consistent direction of a pre-existing frozen signature across several experimental models, alongside explicit demonstrations of incomplete coverage and source limitations. The pattern is not evidence that the observed response is specific to glaucoma, that ECM genes independently outperform the full panel, or that the longer exposure necessarily produces larger changes. The ECM subset is nested and was selected from the same analysis, so apparent differences cannot be interpreted as an unbiased comparison of independent panels.

Existing studies have already characterized shared steroid responses [1], DEX-associated methylation/expression changes [2], responder/nonresponder differences [3], time-dependent TM and Schlemm's canal responses [4], and chromatin-linked disease-associated genes [5]. The recently published PNAS article reports 67 IOP/POAG-linked genes, whereas the earlier preprint title reports 78 [6]. The final-paper S2 data were recovered from the official CC BY PMC Cloud release; exact union of five GWAS sheets and their resource, control and DEX promoter-contact sections reproduced the reported 67 unique genes (57 POAG, 29 IOP, 19 shared), including the reported lead variant counts (46 and 23). None of the 67 stable Ensembl IDs overlapped the frozen 241 genes or its ECM16 subset. This is a descriptive identity comparison of different selection criteria, not a test of independence or enrichment. GSE301517 is the RNA-seq subseries of GSE301525, the same PNAS study. No disease relevance or novelty follows from zero overlap. The fixed 67-member audit against the hash-verified original selection models identified 48 targets tested in both models (19 not in both), 37 significant in both with concordant directions (11 of the 48 did not pass this joint rule), and four passing both absolute log2FC >= 1 thresholds. The other 33 concordant targets did not pass the strong-effect threshold. None was in the frozen 241 set. This demonstrates that exact final-panel non-overlap is not equivalent to absence from the original DEX response. The four internally eligible genes (ENSG00000104537, ENSG00000119699, ENSG00000144749, ENSG00000159713) require examination of the original, hash-matched 48-hour selection source to identify their remaining exclusion reason. No addition to the frozen panel or confirmatory disease-gene claim is made. Donor and pool share the same cohort. See run 45578473621 and evidence/ocular_pnas67_selection_lineage_20261125.json. Do not substitute the preprint candidate count or treat the 7-day dataset as independent of the corresponding publication. The October 19, 2026 erratum to [4] added a data availability section [7]; inspect the corrected source and verify selection-stage supplementary file identity before finalizing the citation lineage.

Important limitations include post-treatment BeamA annotation, condition-specific library effects, same-cohort donor/pool modeling, prior dataset inspection, small and incompletely certified biological sample sets, platform-specific measurement and mapping, and unresolved sample labels. GSE37474 includes mixed tissue and cannot be interpreted as purified TM. The 14-day analysis is conditional; the unresolved metadata conflict is not corrected merely because the observed response is positive. Missing nonresponder samples preclude a clinical classifier or group comparison. The exploratory enrichment and nested panel selection are not confirmatory pathway tests. No independent clinical IOP outcome is linked to this fixed panel.

Future work should first obtain genuinely unexamined, uniformly quantified paired data under an approved design, lock the panel and endpoint before viewing expression, verify sample independence and treatment metadata, and report all negative and missing results. Clinical biomarker and mechanistic studies require separately designed phenotypes and experiments; they are not implications already established here.
