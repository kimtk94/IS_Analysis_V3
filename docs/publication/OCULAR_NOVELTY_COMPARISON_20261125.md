## Source acquisition decision
PubMed PMID43013538 verified final date and count, but article full text and supporting gene list not obtained via public web. NLM retired the old PMC OA Web Service on 2026-08-25. Use official PMC Cloud Service as described in https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/pmcaws/ and https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/README.txt. Query only exact article prefix and official exposed open access metadata objects; verify version and MD5; inventory referenced supplementary files and sheet schema. Do not bypass restrictions, guess workbook contents, or infer gene membership from reported totals. PMC13778663 and corrected PMC13712379 queried separately. No new analysis of a private or embargoed source, no exact 67-gene overlap claimed. 

## Verified final PNAS identity comparison

All 174 records in the five S2 sheets were assigned according to the authors' explicitly labeled resource, control and DEX sections, not selected to match reported counts. Unique stable Ensembl gene IDs reproduced 57 POAG, 29 IOP, 19 shared and 67 union, with 46 and 23 respective lead rsIDs. Resource/control/DEX sets contained 50/41/20 unique genes. Exact overlap with both frozen panels was zero in every condition and phenotype group. See [original CC BY S2](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13778663.1/pnas.2609070123.sd02.xlsx), [run 45578116782](https://github.com/kimtk94/IS_Analysis_V3/actions/runs/45578116782/artifacts/15004188841) and committed evidence. No inferential overlap test: comparable tested universe is not established. Different molecular questions (promoter contact targets versus strong expression changes) explain why non-overlap is possible; the cause of non-overlap is not yet demonstrated. Zero overlap is neither novelty proof nor evidence of disease association, absence of regulation, or exclusion from all DEGs. GSE301517 is part of the same PNAS SuperSeries GSE301525. Do not claim independent replication against the final-paper data. Prioritize a broader, locked 67-target audit of donor/pool and selection-stage 48h estimates and explicit threshold categories, without refitting the 241/16 panel.
