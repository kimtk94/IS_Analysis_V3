## Novelty barriers
- Mishra et al. 2022 Nature https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05165-3 already investigated cross-ancestry allelic heterogeneity, shared SH3PXD2A credible sets (19 overlapping variants), ALDH2 coding and other loci. 
- Seo et al. 2024 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiolchem.2024.108086 already performed cell-type-specific stroke subtype coloc. 2026 PMID 42003665 brain sc/bulk eQTL SMR, MR and coloc.
- Yao et al. 2025 https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.125.050982 East Asian and European cis-pQTL stroke MR, includes FGF5 and ALDH2, colocalization and vascular risk factors.
- 2025 human cerebrovascular multiomics PMC12321221 already implicates COL4A2 rs9515201 in mural/stromal regulation. 2026 Cell https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.07.007 cerebrovascular spatial atlas already stratifies genetic risk by cell state.
- 2023 STROMICS PMC10362040 reported East Asian FGF5/PITX2 risk allele evidence, COL4A2/SH3PXD2A unexpected directions in WGS case vs external reference comparisons; not adjusted independent validation. 2026 Taiwan TPMI PMC13239414 already implicates FGF5 and chr19p13.12 in IS.
- A direct reported-effect contrast is a prioritization screen, not a publication novelty claim. Follow-up only if distinct, independently validated mechanism can be tested with matched-ancestry full QTL and independent EAS phenotype. Treat FGF5/ALDH2 as known comparators, COL4A2/SH3PXD2A as mechanism hypotheses. Prior 36 formal P3A coloc remains HOLD.
