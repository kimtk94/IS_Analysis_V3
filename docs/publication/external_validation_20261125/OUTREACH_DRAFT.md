Subject: Feasibility of aggregate genetic association validation across ischemic stroke subtypes

Dear GENESIS-K research team,

We are conducting an exploratory reanalysis of publicly available GIGASTROKE summary statistics to examine genetic effect heterogeneity across ischemic stroke subtypes and ancestries. We would like to ask whether an independent, appropriately authorized aggregate validation could be feasible in your cohort.

We have fixed a set of 24 markers before obtaining external data. We are particularly interested in the availability of adjudicated large-artery, cardioembolic and small-vessel stroke phenotypes, suitable stroke-free controls, and genotype or imputation coverage of the exact requested allele pairs. If the study design and data-sharing approvals permit, we would appreciate aggregate association estimates (signed beta, standard error, P, effect allele frequency, case/control and marker-specific counts, and imputation quality) for all 24 markers, including unavailable or QC-failed markers. We would also need the phenotype definitions, ancestry/model specifications, recruitment period and any overlap with the original GIGASTROKE cohorts.

We are not requesting individual-level data. If only within-case subtype analyses are available, we would first discuss a separate, appropriately specified study rather than interpret those as case-control replication. We would be happy to share our frozen marker table and proposed analysis protocol for review and discuss feasible collaboration under your governance procedures.

Thank you for considering this inquiry.
