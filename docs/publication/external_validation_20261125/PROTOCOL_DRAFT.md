# Proposed external replication protocol — version 0.1, no external effects observed

The retrospective fixed-family discovery analyses used 192 contrasts in one GIGASTROKE release. This packet does not make those analyses prospective. An external family must be finalized before observing external estimates.

1. **Endpoint decision before readout.** Maintain the earlier 24-marker AIS family for comparable all-IS association transportability; the primary scientific priority for a subtype-qualified cohort is a separate 24-marker LAS case-control association family. Select one primary family for each proposed cohort before obtaining effects based on design, not results. If claiming multiple families jointly, adjust across their total tests or predefine a valid hierarchical procedure before readout; do not apply 0.05/24 to multiple families and treat overall FWER as 0.05. CES/SVS are requested for contextual reporting and a separate prespecified family if eligible, not automatically new confirmatory tests. Related Phecode infarction is not exact AIS; case-only contrasts are not case-control effects. 
2. **Source gates.** Document consent and lawful aggregate access, exact phenotype and case/control definitions, recruitment and non-overlap, model and covariates, genotype/imputation quality, and per-marker N. Unknown overlap or incompatible phenotype/model => descriptive or HOLD. Freeze model-specific quality thresholds with investigators without inspecting effect values. 
3. **Identity.** Require exact build/coordinate, allele pair and rsID; no automatic strand complement or rsID-only match. Use exact C/T for rs7203740. Orient beta and EAF to requested effect allele when returned allele pair is uniquely reversed; SE and P unchanged. Keep unresolved/missing in denominator. Require finite beta and SE>0, 0<P<=1 (underflow separately document), 0<EAF<1 and declared frequency population, valid N and QC metric. Preserve raw and harmonized values separately. 
4. **Direction and testing.** For selected family, compare to corresponding archived GIGASTROKE EAS phenotype direction, not AIS direction when testing LAS. If appropriate and prelocked, two-sided P < .05/24 and concordant sign, report all 24 with CIs and unavailable reasons. Do not treat missing or opposite direction as zero. Associations in external EAS alone do not independently verify an EAS–EUR difference. For subtype/ancestry heterogeneity require matched phenotypes/scales and suitable covariance/independent comparator; otherwise descriptive only. No formal MR or colocalization.
5. **Feasibility.** Prior planning scenario (not actual power) 1,400/2,400/5,400 LAS cases for full/75%/50% discovery effect at 80% power, 4 controls per case, hypothetical control EAF .0596, comparable log-odds and 24 tests. GENESIS-K 5,328 total is not subtype N. Verify adequate sample size and non-overlap first. 
6. **Data handling.** Obtain aggregate estimates through approved channel only. No private records, raw genotypes or credentials in GitHub/Actions. No outreach, data permission, external effects, independent replication or main merge established.
