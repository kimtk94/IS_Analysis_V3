### Why the existing analysis cannot yet be a new HCC discovery
- Ng already studied CNA/RNA/protein correlations, oncogenic/metabolic programs and etiology/stages. Suter already performs full multi-omics subtyping. Gao 2019 Cell has 159 HBV paired patients, metabolism, prognostic proteomic clusters. Jiang 2019 Nature and Xing 2023 Cell Reports Medicine also report independent proteomic subtypes and cohort prognosis; Xing validated Ng among several cohorts. Cheng 2023 Briefings in Bioinformatics already reports 158 assay-eligible /156 integrated HBV HCC and 408 concordantly dysregulated RNA-protein genes/three clusters. Simply reproducing rho 0.287 or creating another differential gene list, tumor protein cluster, survival signature or common lipid signal is **prior art**, not claimed novelty.
- Ng ALD-only N=12 and HCV-only N=11 for the 48-patient Suter subset; sex standardized difference 0.667 and early-stage BCLC SMD 0.260. An etiology interaction within these small, imbalanced groups is EXPLORATORY at best, no primary confirmatory disease-specific mechanism.
- Xing 2023 proteome and source clinical metadata available (152 matched clinical labels) but outcome event codebook/time zero/censoring still not validated and full RNA matched matrix not proven. Cannot run a meaningful external RNA–protein replication or recurrence prediction solely from proteome+ambiguous RFS codes.
- GSE149614 single-cell ~10 HCC patients is cellular context potential, not independent patient-level validation of a proteomics estimate.
- Clinical model improvement or radiomics must not be bolted on unless true paired patient imaging/endpoints and sample-level joins exist; otherwise avoid claim.

## B. ONE primary research objective and alternatives

**Proposed conditional core question (RQ1 re-scoped):** Are specific tumor RNA-to-protein discordance *pathways* robust across independently sourced matched HCC cohorts, and is between-cohort/etiology heterogeneity biologically interpretable after assay, tumor purity, sampling site and patient-level dependence are controlled?

Primary estimands (only after QA):
1. Patient-matched **within-cohort gene-wise rho** across individual patients and prespecified pathway-level summary of cross-cohort effect sizes, **not** the pooled 0.284 median itself.
2. Cross-study concordance of gene-wise effects in an independently matched HCC RNA/protein cohort, with confidence intervals, detection/missingness sensitivity and heterogeneity.
3. In externally annotated scRNA-seq patients, cell-compartment attribution of prespecified pathways via *patient-level pseudobulk*; scRNA cannot directly measure protein abundance and must not be labeled multiomics replication.
4. Etiology interaction (ALD/HCV etc) **ONLY IF** independent etiologic diversity, adequate power and batch/sex/stage adjustment become available. HBV-only Gao cannot validate ALD-vs-HCV interaction.

**Fallback** if independent matched RNA/protein fails: prespecified cross-cohort protein/pathway direction triangulation (not same-patient multi-omics) and modest exploratory report; explicitly remove claim of independent proteotranscriptomic replication. If no new direction beyond Ng/Suter/Xing/Gao/Cheng, STOP/RETOPIC rather than manufacture novelty.
